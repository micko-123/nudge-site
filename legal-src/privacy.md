## 1. The short version

Nudge keeps your financial data on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and we
cannot see your transactions, balances, goals or debts. The only things that leave your phone are
described in section 4.

## 2. Data you enter

Accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, debts, recurring items, settings, XP and achievements are stored in a
database on your device. The app does not upload this data. If you turn on App Lock, the database is
encrypted, and the key is stored in your device's secure storage.

## 3. Permissions

- **Notifications** (optional): bill, streak, budget and backup reminders are scheduled on your device.
  Notification text does not include amounts unless you turn that on. The permission is requested only when
  you first turn on a reminder.
- **Biometrics or PIN** (optional): used only to unlock the app. Biometric data stays in your device's
  system and is never seen by Nudge.
- **Files**: only when you choose to import a CSV or restore a backup, using the system file picker.
- **Microphone** (optional): for "Say it". The sound goes to your phone's speech recognition service (for
  example Google's), which may process it online under its own terms. Only the recognised words reach Nudge,
  and they stay on your phone.
- **Camera and photos** (optional): a receipt photo is taken by your camera app or by Google's document
  scanner, or picked from your gallery. It is read on your phone and kept in the app's storage with the
  entry (and in backups you make).

## 4. What leaves your phone

| What | To whom | Why | Your data included? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ad requests | Google AdMob | Show ads that fund the app | Advertising ID, device and ad-interaction data collected by the AdMob SDK. No financial data. |
| Exchange-rate request | open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API) | Daily currency rates | No. It is a plain request for rates, and like any internet request it exposes your IP address to that provider. |
| App settings file | A static file on GitHub Pages | Ad settings, and news of an app update | No user data is sent. It is a plain request for one file. |
| Crypto prices (optional, only if you turn on automatic crypto prices) | CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) | Today's price of the coins you hold, at most once a day | Only the coin names and currency codes. Never amounts or holdings. |
| Backups you share | The app you pick (Drive, email, etc.) | Your own backup | Only when you choose to export and send a file. |
| Google on-device helpers (optional, only after you agree) | Google (ML Kit, through Google Play services) | Download Google's document scanner and a text-reading model (about 6 MB), once | No. Your photos and messages are processed on your phone. Like other Google Play services, ML Kit may send Google usage and diagnostic information under Google's terms. You can turn each helper off in Settings → Google on-device helpers. |
| Voice entry (optional) | Your phone's speech service (for example Google) | Turn speech into words | Your voice, handled by that service under its own terms. Nudge itself sends nothing. |

We do not sell your data, and we do not receive it.

## 5. Advertising and consent

Nudge shows ads through Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions that require
it, we show Google's consent form before the first ad request. You can change your choice any time in
Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences. If you decline, you may see non-personalised ads. Learn how Google uses
data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset
your advertising ID in your device settings.

Nudge is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## 6. Backups

- **Manual backups** are files you create and send where you choose. You are responsible for keeping them safe.
  An optional password encrypts the file; if you lose the password we cannot recover it.
- **Local auto-backups** are kept in the app's storage on your device.
- **Android Auto Backup**: Android may back up the app's data to your Google account if you have device
  backup on. That is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us. To remove it, manage backups in
  your Google account settings.

## 7. Crash reports and analytics

Version 1.0 has no analytics and no crash-reporting library. Google Play may give us aggregated crash and
performance statistics (Android vitals) that contain no financial data from the app.

## 8. Your choices and deletion

- Settings → Reset everything deletes the database, in-app backups, scheduled notifications and XP, and
  restarts onboarding.
- Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Copies in your own backup files or Android Auto
  Backup stay until you delete them.
- Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

## 9. Security

Data stays on your device, protected by your device's lock, and optionally by App Lock and database
encryption. No method is perfectly secure; keep your device updated and use a screen lock.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, the app shows a short summary of what changed and asks you to continue. The version
number above is stored on your device.
